Run João's weekly review (GTD-style).

1. Inbox: confirm every capture this week was filed.

5. SOMEDAY: surface 1–2 ideas worth taking forward now and say why.

7. Propose next week's three priorities (one sentence each).
8. Save the review to brain/daily/YYYY-Www-review.md. Commit and push.

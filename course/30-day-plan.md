# The First 30 Days

A day-by-day plan for putting the method into practice. Most days need 10–20 minutes. The heavier days are marked.

If you miss a day, don't double up. Pick up where you are.

---

## Week 1 — Get clear

| Day | Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the [course overview](README.md) and [Module 0](modules/00-orientation.md). Complete your baseline. | 30 min |
| 2 | Notice busy-but-not-useful moments. Write a word each time. | Through the day |
| 3 | Read [Module 1](modules/01-planning-as-design.md). Name your five to seven areas. | 20 min |
| 4 | Write a vision and current standard for each area. | **40 min** |
| 5 | Add a role for each area. Score the gap. Choose your lead area. | 20 min |
| 6 | Read your Life Map before opening messages. Read [Module 2](modules/02-why-lists-fail.md). | 20 min |
| 7 | Do the list autopsy. Start the Reactivity Audit. | 20 min |

## Week 2 — Learn to plan

| Day | Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Reactivity Audit, evening. Read [Module 3](modules/03-the-three-questions.md). | 25 min |
| 9 | Write your first full plan — lead area. | **30 min** |
| 10 | Write two more plans — work and personal. Run all three through the five tests. | **30 min** |
| 11 | Do the task translation on ten list items. | 20 min |
| 12 | Before every block over 30 minutes, write *Result, Reason, Route* in the margin. | Through the day |
| 13 | Finish the Reactivity Audit. Write down your three patterns. | 20 min |
| 14 | Read [Module 4](modules/04-capture-to-commitment.md). Book your weekly session as a recurring appointment. | 20 min |

## Week 3 — Build the rhythm

| Day | Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Your first Weekly Mise en Place.** Use the [template](tools/weekly-mise-en-place.md). | **60 min** |
| 16 | First [Daily Card](tools/daily-card.md). First protected block. | 10 min + block |
| 17 | Daily Card. Protected block. Evening close. | 15 min + block |
| 18 | Daily Card. Read [Module 5](modules/05-leverage.md). | 30 min |
| 19 | Daily Card. Do the time audit. | **40 min** |
| 20 | Daily Card. Build your Leverage Ledger — five items. | 30 min |
| 21 | Write one handover brief. Book the conversation. | 20 min |

## Week 4 — Protect it

| Day | Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | **Weekly Mise en Place.** Note how it compares with the first. | **60 min** |
| 23 | Daily Card. Have the handover conversation. | 10 min + conversation |
| 24 | Daily Card. Read [Module 6](modules/06-time-focus-state.md). Set your time targets. | 30 min |
| 25 | Daily Card. Book the minimums for the next four weeks. | 20 min |
| 26 | Daily Card. Build your Restoratives Menu — 15 items. Book one main. | 25 min |
| 27 | Daily Card. Sketch your ideal working day. | 20 min |
| 28 | Daily Card. Read [Module 7](modules/07-the-long-game.md). Write your rules for winning. | 30 min |
| 29 | **Weekly Mise en Place.** Book the review rhythm: 13 weekly, 3 monthly, 1 quarterly. | **60 min** |
| 30 | Refine your Life Map. Score your baseline again. Compare. | **40 min** |

---

## After day 30

Keep three things and you'll keep the method:

1. **The Daily Card**, every working morning
2. **The Weekly Mise en Place**, same time every week
3. **The Quarterly Review**, booked 13 weeks out

Everything else supports those three.

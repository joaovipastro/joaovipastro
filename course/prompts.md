# Thinking-Partner Prompts

Sixty prompts for working through the method with an AI assistant, a coach or a trusted colleague. Paste your own notes in where you see brackets.

A general instruction that helps with any AI tool: *"Act as an experienced executive coach. Ask me questions before giving advice. Challenge my assumptions where they're weak. Keep your answers short."*

---

## Getting clear (Modules 0–1)

1. Here are my notes on where my time went this week: [notes]. What patterns do you see that I might be missing?
2. Interview me, one question at a time, to help me write a vision for this area of my life: [area].
3. Here's my draft Life Map: [map]. Which visions sound generic? Help me make them specific to me.
4. I've named these areas: [list]. Is anything important missing? Is anything overlapping?
5. Here's the current standard I wrote for [area]: [text]. Am I being too harsh, too generous, or about right?
6. Which of these areas, if improved, would most lift the others? Make the case for two different answers: [map].
7. Help me find the right role name for who I am at my best in [area]. Offer five options with a line on each.

## Seeing the problem (Module 2)

8. Here's my current to-do list: [list]. Sort it into results, maintenance, other people's priorities, and items I should delete.
9. Here are my Reactivity Audit notes for the week: [notes]. What three patterns stand out?
10. I keep saying I don't have time for [thing]. Ask me questions until we find out what's really going on.
11. When in my week am I most likely to be reactive, based on this: [calendar or notes]? What would you change first?
12. Rewrite these five tasks as the results they serve: [tasks].

## Writing plans (Module 3)

13. Here's a Result I've written: [result]. Test it against these five criteria: outcome, specific, mine, right-sized, pulls me. Then suggest a stronger version.
14. I want to [vague goal]. Help me turn it into a Result I could achieve in [timeframe].
15. Here's my Reason for this Result: [reason]. It feels flat. Ask me questions that might uncover a stronger one.
16. Help me write four layers of Reason for this Result — what it gives me, what it gives others, what it spares me, what it says about who I am: [result].
17. Brainstorm every action that could contribute to this Result. Then tell me which three or four would produce most of it: [result].
18. Here's my Route: [route]. What's missing? What could I cut?
19. Which of these actions should I not be doing myself? [route]
20. Here's a task that's been on my list for weeks: [task]. Ask *"what for?"* until we reach something meaningful — or decide it can go.

## The weekly ritual (Module 4)

21. Here's everything I captured this week: [list]. Group it by the result each item serves. Separate maintenance and later items.
22. Here are my groups for the week: [groups]. Draft a Result and two-line Reason for each.
23. My musts add up to [X] hours and I have [Y] free. Help me decide what to cut, and explain the trade-offs.
24. Turn this weekly plan into a calendar outline with protected must-time blocks: [plan + fixed commitments].
25. My week just fell apart because [reason]. Help me rebuild it in five minutes: what's the one must I protect today?
26. I skipped my weekly session for three weeks. Walk me through a 20-minute reset.

## Leverage (Module 5)

27. Here's how I spent my time last week: [list with hours]. Which of these is only-me work, and which isn't? Be honest.
28. For each activity here, suggest the best lever — stop, simplify, systemise, or hand over — and why: [list].
29. Help me write a handover brief for [activity] to [person]: Result, Reason, Boundaries, Resources, Check-in.
30. I keep doing [task] myself because [reason]. Challenge that reasoning.
31. Draft a one-page checklist for [recurring process] that someone new could follow.
32. What could I automate in this process, using tools a small business is likely to have? [process]
33. What would happen if I simply stopped doing [activity]? Think through second-order effects.
34. I've freed [X] hours a week. Help me decide what to put them towards, using my Life Map: [map].

## Time, focus and state (Module 6)

35. Here's my Life Map: [map]. Suggest minimum and ideal weekly time targets for each area, given that I work [pattern].
36. Design an ideal working day for me based on what I've told you about when I'm sharpest: [notes].
37. Help me build a Restoratives Menu. Ask me about the moments I've felt most like myself this year, then turn the answers into small plates, mains and tasting menus.
38. I'm stuck and irritable right now. Pick one small plate from my menu that fits and tell me to go do it: [menu].
39. My role requires a lot of responsiveness. How can I protect focused time without letting clients or guests down?
40. Here's my current message-checking habit: [description]. Suggest a better rhythm and how to explain it to my team.

## The long game (Module 7)

41. Help me write five rules for winning that are fully within my control.
42. Here are my last four weekly plans: [plans]. What patterns do you see? Which Results keep rolling over, and why might that be?
43. Run my Quarterly Review with me. Ask one question at a time, starting with what went well.
44. Here's my Life Map from three months ago and my notes from this quarter: [both]. Help me update it.
45. Suggest one or two quarterly Results per area, based on this map and these priorities: [map + notes].
46. I keep abandoning planning systems around week six. Help me design a version of this method I'll keep doing on my worst week.
47. Help me write a one-paragraph annual vision for [area], as if it's already a year from now and it went well.

## For leaders

48. Help me teach the three questions to my team in a 20-minute meeting. Draft the agenda.
49. Turn this team goal into a Result, Reason and Route the whole team can see and own: [goal].
50. Here's a project one of my team is struggling with: [description]. Help me coach them through the three questions instead of giving them the answer.

## Drawing on the books

51. Here's a Result I keep putting off: [result]. Help me write both sides of the Reason — what I gain if I do it, and what it costs me over a year if I don't.
52. Run my capture list through the 6-D sort — Discard, Delegate, Do, Date, Drawer, Deter: [list].
53. Triage today's list into P1–P4 and tell me which P2 I must protect: [list].
54. Help me draft a Not-To-Do list for someone in my role: [role]. Then challenge me on which items I'll find hardest to keep.
55. Help me say no to this request, warmly and clearly, in under 60 words: [request].
56. Here are my top clients by revenue and by time spent: [lists]. Build my 80/20 map and suggest one decision per box.
57. Who could I learn from to shortcut this goal? Suggest the kinds of people, books or businesses to study: [goal].
58. Something has gone wrong: [situation]. Walk me through the five problem-solving questions, one at a time.
59. Help me rank my values and write rules for each that make it easy to feel I'm living them.
60. Guide me through a three-minute morning rehearsal of today's three Results: [results].
